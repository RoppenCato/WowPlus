# Stories our quests have told

One line per story, so that the next quests for a zone tell something else. The quest-writing skill (`.claude/skills/write-quests`) reads this before choosing a story and adds to it afterwards.

| Zone | What it tells | Quests |
|---|---|---|
| Mulgore | **The Hatred of the Centaur.** Why the tauren wandered for generations, how centaur make war (scouts first), that Baine was once taken by centaur and brought home by Rexxar, and that the orcs' help is why Mulgore is theirs. Uses the six Galak centaur east of Bloodhoof Village, which no other quest touches. Invented: that Hulfnar Stonetotem's father was a nomad, and that the Galak mark their bracers with a clan knot. | 9200002, 9200003, 9200004 |
| Mulgore | **The White Stag and the Moon.** The hunt as the Earthmother's gift; Apa'ro the white stag whom no hunter could catch, his antlers caught in the stars; Mu'sha the moon (the Earthmother's left eye) who freed and loved him; their child Cenarius, who taught the tauren the first druidism. Told by Vorn Skyseer, ended by the druid trainer Gennia Runetotem. Invented: that feathers are burned to speak to Mu'sha, that the stars "have never lain straight since", and that old kodo bulls left behind by the herd are given a clean death as the hunter's mercy. | 9200005, 9200006, 9200007 |
| Elwynn Forest | **The Candle and the Dark.** What kobolds are (Brann Bronzebeard's claim that they came of troggs), that the dark is death to them ("the darkness took them"), that the Elwynn clan walked here from Redridge, and that the Riverpaw gnolls have enslaved them and pass the gold on to the Defias. Overlaps on purpose with the game's own mine and candle quests, which never say any of it. Invented: Edrin Halloway and his eleven years in the Fargodeep, Yarrik Whipjaw, and that burned-down candles show the kobolds are kept at work. | 9200008, 9200009, 9200010 |
| Elwynn Forest (road to Westfall) | Stormwind has pulled its soldiers out of Westfall; the People's Militia under Gryan Stoutmantle are farmers with what arms they could find. Invented: that Westbrook Garrison quietly spares arms. | 9200011 |
| Elwynn Forest (road to Redridge) | Lakeshire is pressed by Blackrock orcs in Stonewatch Keep and by gnolls, and Stormwind sends no soldiers. Invented: Drake Lindgren's wagons. | 9200012 |
| Dun Morogh | **The Ones Who Were Here First.** From the wiki (Frostmane tribe, Ironforge kingdom): the Frostmane are ice trolls of Drakkari descent who left Northrend after the Sundering; they once ruled Dun Morogh and lost it to the dwarves, who came west from Uldaman, conquered the area from the ice trolls and built Ironforge; now they try to take it back while Ironforge is busy with troggs and wars, and the Senate sent Grelin and Senir Whitebeard; shimmerweed on Shimmer Ridge is for their rites; Great Father Arctikus is their patriarch at Frostmane Hold. Overlaps on purpose with the game's Frostmane quests, which only count heads. Invented: Yori Crackhelm's curiosity, and Jin'zakk the Rememberer, an elder who stirs up the young (an enemy to end the chain on). | 9200013, 9200014 |
| Dun Morogh (road to Loch Modan) | From the wiki (Trogg, Gol'Bolar Quarry, Loch Modan): troggs lay shut in Uldaman for thousands of years until dwarven excavation woke them; they have overrun the Gol'Bolar dig and mine; in Loch Modan troggs "unearthed by the dwarven digs" hold the hills, Captain Rugelfuss at the Valley of Kings sends dwarves against them, and the Dark Irons threaten the Stonewrought Dam. Invented: that Gehn sends a warning. | 9200015 |
| Teldrassil | **Once of Our Blood.** From the wiki (Satyr, Gnarlpine tribe, Zenn Foulhoof): Xavius, a Highborne, was remade by Sargeras as the first satyr and turned other willing Highborne; satyrs can still pass the curse on; they manipulate, feed on corruption and spread it, elsewhere among furbolgs. The Gnarlpine went berserk from fel moss and took Starbreeze Village, driving out or killing its people. The player hears Zenn Foulhoof speak for himself. Invented: Zenn's words (the quest leaves open whether they are true), and Xalthas the Gentle, a satyr watching Starbreeze (an enemy to end the chain on; the quest does not make him the cause of the Gnarlpine's madness). | 9200016, 9200017 |
| Teldrassil (road to Darkshore) | From the wiki (Darkshore, Thundris Windweaver, Blackwood tribe, Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde): Ameth'Aran and Bashal'Aran were twin Highborne cities ruined in the War of the Ancients; the Blackwood furbolgs are corrupted (Thundris does not yet know by what); sea creatures wash ashore and the Temple of the Moon looks into it; Thundris is Auberdine's elder, and his greeting is the game's own. Invented: that Daryn Lightwind sends the player to him. | 9200018 |
| Durotar | **The Admiral's War.** From the wiki (Daelin Proudmoore, Tiragarde Keep, Lieutenant Benedict, Echo Isles, the Warcraft III orc campaign): Proudmoore, Lord Admiral of Kul Tiras, commanded the Alliance fleets in the Second War and lost his eldest son Derek and the Third Fleet to dragon riders at Crestfall; after the Third War he sailed to Kalimdor seeking his daughter and attacked the Horde; his ships drove the newly settled Darkspear from the Echo Isles, Rexxar and the batriders buying time to evacuate; he built Tiragarde Keep in haste; the Horde took his mainland base and he fell back to Theramore; Jaina pointed Thrall to ships and asked that her people be spared; Rexxar killed him and Thrall left Theramore in peace; the Darkspear went home, Zalazane betrayed them, and Vol'jin founded Sen'jin Village. The men in the keep now are his reserve under Lieutenant Benedict, who retook it, following orders written before the Admiral left Kul Tiras and expecting Lieutenant Alverold's fleet, which never came. Durotar is named for Durotan and Orgrimmar for Orgrim Doomhammer. Overlaps on purpose with the game's Tiragarde quests. Invented: Magga's interest, Quartermaster Tidewell and his letter (its content repeats what the wiki says of Benedict's orders). | 9200019, 9200020, 9200021 |
| Durotar (road to the Barrens) | From the wiki (The Barrens, Sergra Darkthorn): the Barrens was once a forest tended by the night elves, scorched after the Sundering; the tauren hunted it with the centaur as their sworn enemy; Kolkar centaur and Razormane quilboar; the Crossroads is the largest Horde outpost and Sergra Darkthorn is in charge of it; her greeting is the game's own. Invented: that Vel'rin Fang sends the player. | 9200022 |
| Tirisfal Glades | **What Sleeps Beneath.** From the wiki (Council of Tirisfal, Venomweb Vale, High elf, Tirisfal Glades): about 2,700 years ago the magi of Dalaran and Quel'Thalas formed a secret order against the demons Dalaran's magic had let in; it met in Venomweb Vale, where a Council member created the spiders "in a mad bid to secure the meeting place"; Medivh was the last Guardian. About 7,000 years ago the exiled Highborne under Dath'Remar Sunstrider settled in Tirisfal among primitive human tribes, met shades of dark energy that drove some mad, believed something evil slept beneath (never proven) and went north to found Quel'Thalas. Invented: Claire Willower's parish records, and that the Vile Fin murlocs have picked up cups the elves left (something to fetch). | 9200023, 9200024 |
| Tirisfal Glades (Faol's Rest) | From the wiki (Alonsus Faol): Archbishop of the Church of the Holy Light; after the Northshire clerics were massacred he proposed a new order to Anduin Lothar at the start of the Second War and trained it with his apprentice Uther, the first paladin; he died before the Third War and is buried at Faol's Rest. Invented: Dark Cleric Beryl's regard for him. | 9200025 |
| Tirisfal Glades (road to Silverpine) | From the wiki (Silverpine Forest, Arugal, Pyrewood Village): Arugal summoned the worgen against the Scourge, they turned on the living, and he went mad and adopted them in Shadowfang Keep; he cursed Pyrewood, whose folk are worgen by night; Dalaran's mages hold Ambermill; Thule Ravenclaw and the Rot Hide gnolls are on Fenris Isle; High Executor Hadrec commands at the Sepulcher. Invented: Deathguard Linnea's message. | 9200026 |

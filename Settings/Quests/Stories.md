# Stories our quests have told

One line per story, so that the next quests for a zone tell something else. The quest-writing skill (`.claude/skills/write-quests`) reads this before choosing a story and adds to it afterwards.

| Zone | What it tells | Quests |
|---|---|---|
| Mulgore | **The Hatred of the Centaur.** Why the tauren wandered for generations, how centaur make war (scouts first), that Baine was once taken by centaur and brought home by Rexxar, and that the orcs' help is why Mulgore is theirs. Uses the six Galak centaur east of Bloodhoof Village, which no other quest touches. Invented: that Hulfnar Stonetotem's father was a nomad, and that the Galak mark their bracers with a clan knot. | 9200002, 9200003, 9200004 |
| Mulgore | **The White Stag and the Moon.** The hunt as the Earthmother's gift; Apa'ro the white stag whom no hunter could catch, his antlers caught in the stars; Mu'sha the moon (the Earthmother's left eye) who freed and loved him; their child Cenarius, who taught the tauren the first druidism. Told by Vorn Skyseer, ended by the druid trainer Gennia Runetotem. Invented: that feathers are burned to speak to Mu'sha, that the stars "have never lain straight since", and that old kodo bulls left behind by the herd are given a clean death as the hunter's mercy. | 9200005, 9200006, 9200007 |
| Elwynn Forest | **The Candle and the Dark.** What kobolds are (Brann Bronzebeard's claim that they came of troggs), that the dark is death to them ("the darkness took them"), that the Elwynn clan walked here from Redridge, and that the Riverpaw gnolls have enslaved them and pass the gold on to the Defias. Overlaps on purpose with the game's own mine and candle quests, which never say any of it. Invented: Edrin Halloway and his eleven years in the Fargodeep, Yarrik Whipjaw, and that burned-down candles show the kobolds are kept at work. | 9200008, 9200009, 9200010 |
| Elwynn Forest (road to Westfall) | Stormwind has pulled its soldiers out of Westfall; the People's Militia under Gryan Stoutmantle are farmers with what arms they could find. Invented: that Westbrook Garrison quietly spares arms. | 9200011 |
| Elwynn Forest (road to Redridge) | Lakeshire is pressed by Blackrock orcs in Stonewatch Keep and by gnolls, and Stormwind sends no soldiers. Invented: Drake Lindgren's wagons. | 9200012 |
| Dun Morogh | **The Ones Who Were Here First.** The Frostmane are ice trolls who hunted Dun Morogh before the dwarves came west out of Uldaman and cut Ironforge, and the war between them is two and a half thousand years old. Overlaps on purpose with the game's Frostmane quests, which only count heads. Invented: that the seers on Shimmer Ridge keep the tribe's memory, Jin'zakk the Rememberer, and Yori Crackhelm's curiosity. | 9200013, 9200014 |
| Dun Morogh (road to Loch Modan) | The troggs were shut under the stone and are let out by the Explorers' League's own digging, at Gol'Bolar as at Ironband's Excavation; in Loch Modan troggs hold the hills and the Dark Irons eye the Stonewrought Dam. | 9200015 |
| Teldrassil | **Once of Our Blood.** What a satyr is: Xavius, counsellor to Queen Azshara, remade by Sargeras, and the Highborne who took his curse by choice. The player hears Zenn Foulhoof speak for himself. Invented: that Zenn is one of the first satyrs, Xalthas the Gentle, and that a satyr tends the Gnarlpine's madness at Starbreeze Village (the game puts it down to the corruption of the tree). | 9200016, 9200017 |
| Teldrassil (road to Darkshore) | Darkshore's Highborne ruins (Ameth'Aran, Bashal'Aran), the maddened Blackwood furbolgs, the sea creatures washing ashore. Invented: that Darnassus leaves Auberdine's letters unanswered. | 9200018 |
| Durotar | **The Admiral's War.** Why there are humans in Durotar: Daelin Proudmoore of Kul Tiras, whose son died to orc-ridden dragons in the Second War, attacked the new Horde; his fleet burned the Darkspear out of the Echo Isles; Rexxar and Thrall ended it at Theramore while Jaina stood aside; the garrison of Tiragarde was never relieved. Ends with what Durotar and Orgrimmar are named for, and Vol'jin. Overlaps on purpose with the game's Tiragarde quests. Invented: Magga's part at Razor Hill, Quartermaster Tidewell and the letter's words. | 9200019, 9200020, 9200021 |
| Durotar (road to the Barrens) | The Barrens feeds Orgrimmar; quilboar and centaur fought over it before the Horde came; the Crossroads is one town with too few spears. | 9200022 |
| Tirisfal Glades | **What Sleeps Beneath.** The Council of Tirisfal and its Guardians took their name from these glades; and seven thousand years ago the high elves landed here first, some went mad, said something evil slept under the ground, and went north to found Quel'Thalas. Invented: Claire Willower's parish records, the stillness of Stillwater Pond, and that the Vile Fin murlocs hoard what the elves left. | 9200023, 9200024 |
| Tirisfal Glades (Faol's Rest) | Archbishop Alonsus Faol founded the Knights of the Silver Hand in the Second War, with Uther the first paladin; the Forsaken turned from his Light to the Shadow. | 9200025 |
| Tirisfal Glades (road to Silverpine) | Silverpine: Arugal in Shadowfang Keep and his worgen, Dalaran at Ambermill, Pyrewood Village by night, and the Forsaken holding it thinly. | 9200026 |

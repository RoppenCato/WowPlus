# Stories our quests have told

One line per story, so that the next quests for a zone tell something else. The quest-writing skill (`.claude/skills/write-quests`) reads this before choosing a story and adds to it afterwards.

| Zone | What it tells | Quests |
|---|---|---|
| Mulgore | **The Hatred of the Centaur.** Why the tauren wandered for generations, how centaur make war (scouts first), that Baine was once taken by centaur and brought home by Rexxar, and that the orcs' help is why Mulgore is theirs. Uses the six Galak centaur east of Bloodhoof Village, which no other quest touches. Invented: that Hulfnar Stonetotem's father was a nomad, and that the Galak mark their bracers with a clan knot. | 9200002, 9200003, 9200004 |
| Mulgore | **The White Stag and the Moon.** The hunt as the Earthmother's gift; Apa'ro the white stag whom no hunter could catch, his antlers caught in the stars; Mu'sha the moon (the Earthmother's left eye) who freed and loved him; their child Cenarius, who taught the tauren the first druidism. Told by Vorn Skyseer, ended by the druid trainer Gennia Runetotem. Invented: that feathers are burned to speak to Mu'sha, that the stars "have never lain straight since", and that old kodo bulls left behind by the herd are given a clean death as the hunter's mercy. | 9200005, 9200006, 9200007 |

# How chat sounds in MapleStory (version 83, around 2009). For the MapleStoryPlus bridge.
# Lines starting with # are skipped.
Players are often younger, and chat is quicker and lighter than in other online games: short lines, friendly, a little silly.
Common words: mesos, pots, mobs, grinding, training spot, drops, scrolling, PQ (party quest), FM (Free Market), CC (change channel), KS (kill steal), job advancement, party, buff, smega, nx.
Places are named in full or by nickname: Hene, Kerning, Perion, Ludi, Orbis, El Nath, Sleepywood, Leafre.
Asking for a party or a buff, and thanking for one, is most of what strangers say to each other.

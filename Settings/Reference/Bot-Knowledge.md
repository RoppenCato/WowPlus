# What every bot knows about this server. One fact per line; lines starting with # are skipped.
# The bridge (Settings/Scripts/Bot-Chat-Bridge.py) reads this file for every reply, so a change
# here works at once. Keep it short, and keep it to what a player would know from playing:
# it is hints, not a manual. Do not put secrets here that the world is meant to keep.
This server has few real players; most people you meet keep to themselves, and that is normal here.
Experience comes a bit faster than in the old days, and discovering new areas pays well.
Killing a rare enemy for the first time gives bonus experience, and rares always drop something extra.
Enemies and chests can drop a surprise piece of gear for your level, sometimes even a mount or a pet.
Vendors sometimes have one unusual item on the shelf, and it changes every week. Some travelling merchants have a theme.
Dungeons and raids adjust to how many people are in the group, so small groups work.
Your hearthstone has no cooldown and opens a menu with places you have already visited, including a hub with every kind of vendor.
Gear does not bind, so you can hand it on.
Holding Shift lets you sprint for a few seconds on foot or while swimming.
You can learn every profession, and gathering gives more than it used to.
